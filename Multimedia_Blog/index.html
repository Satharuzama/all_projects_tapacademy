Movie Trailer 1

The official trailer for Animal was released on 23 November 2023 by T-Series and has a runtime of 3 minutes and 32 seconds. It depicts the character arc of Arjun Singh, played by Ranbir Kapoor, from a devoted son of Balbir Singh (Anil Kapoor) to a violent avenger. The trailer features Bobby Deol as the primary antagonist and Rashmika Mandanna as Arjun's wife Geetanjali. It received over 50 million views within 24 hours of its release, making it the third most-viewed Hindi film trailer on YouTube at the time. The film was theatrically released on 1 December 2023 with a runtime of 3 hours and 21 minutes and an A-certificate from the Central Board of Film Certification.

Movie Trailer 2

The official trailer for RRR was released on 9 December 2021 by DVV Danayya and has a runtime of 3 minutes and 16 seconds. It was released simultaneously in five languages — Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada, and Malayalam — with theatrical launch events held in Hyderabad, Mumbai, and Bangalore. The trailer depicts the parallel journeys of Jr NTR as Komaram Bheem and Ram Charan as Alluri Sitarama Raju, two revolutionaries set against the British in 1920s India, with Ajay Devgn and Alia Bhatt appearing in supporting roles. It became the first Indian film trailer to cross 50 million views within 24 hours across all language versions combined, and surpassed 100 million views within eight days. The film was theatrically released on 25 March 2022 with a runtime of 185 minutes and a production budget of approximately ₹400 crore.

Movie Trailer 3

The official trailer for Jawan was released on 31 August 2023 by Red Chillies Entertainment and has a runtime of 2 minutes and 45 seconds. A prevue, titled "Jawan Prevue," had been released earlier on 10 July 2023. The trailer, directed by Atlee, depicts Shah Rukh Khan in dual roles — a rugged avenger and a charismatic father — alongside Vijay Sethupathi as a weapons smuggler and Nayanthara as an investigative police officer, with Deepika Padukone appearing in a special cameo. The trailer received a U/A certificate from the Central Board of Film Certification. The film was theatrically released on 7 September 2023 with a runtime of 169 minutes and a production budget of approximately ₹300 crore.

Movie Trailer 4

Unlike conventional releases, The Paradise did not have a traditional theatrical trailer. Director Srikanth Odela and star Nani confirmed in a press meet on 11 September 2026 that the team collectively decided to skip a trailer, stating that completing the film's post-production was more important at that stage. The only promotional video released was an official teaser with a runtime of 1 minute and 42 seconds, launched in August 2026 simultaneously in Telugu, Tamil, Hindi, Kannada, and Malayalam. Srikanth Odela stated that releasing the film without a trailer would allow audiences to experience more surprises, comparing it to the experience of walking into a theatre knowing nothing about a film. The film was theatrically released on 24 September 2026 with a runtime of 2 hours and 49 minutes and an A-certificate, grossing a reported ₹76.90 crore worldwide on its opening day

Movie Trailer 5

The official trailer for Pushpa 2: The Rule was released on 17 November 2024 by Mythri Movie Makers and has a runtime of 2 minutes and 44 seconds. It was launched at a grand pre-release event held at Gandhi Maidan in Patna, Bihar, a region where the first installment enjoyed massive popularity. A teaser had been released earlier on 8 April 2024 to coincide with Allu Arjun's birthday. The trailer was released simultaneously in six languages — Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, and Bengali — and garnered approximately 4.3 million views in Telugu and 2.9 million in Hindi within the first hour of its release. The film was theatrically released on 5 December 2024 with a runtime of 200 minutes, a U/A certificate from the Central Board of Film Certification, and a production budget of ₹400–500 crore, making it one of the most expensive Indian films ever produced.
